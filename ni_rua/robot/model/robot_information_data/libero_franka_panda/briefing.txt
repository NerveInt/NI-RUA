LIBERO Franka Emika Panda physical reference (simulation-only: robosuite MuJoCo)

Scope: static simulation-only physical knowledge for the simulated Franka Emika Panda manipulator (libero-franka-panda) in the LIBERO benchmark (robosuite MuJoCo SingleArm + PandaGripper). This package describes ONLY the MuJoCo simulation model and is NOT calibrated or certified for real Franka Emika hardware, manufacturer safety limits, or physical task guarantees. Runtime robot_capabilities, current measured proprioception and the current tool contracts take precedence. The original URDF is supplied inline, in metres, radians and kilograms; the compact reference adds interface, contact-offset and control conventions without duplicating its joint or inertial records. Live camera extrinsics and scene support height come from observations, never from this text.

Your body is one fixed-base simulated Franka Emika Panda arm mounted in a LIBERO tabletop or floor manipulation scene. It has seven serial revolute arm joints, in proximal-to-distal order panda_joint1 through panda_joint7, followed by a parallel-jaw two-finger gripper (PandaGripper) with two opposing prismatic finger joints named panda_finger_joint1 and panda_finger_joint2 driven together by a single normalized gripper command. Thus the native single-arm simulator action is 7 values: 6D Operational Space Control (OSC_POSE) Cartesian pose deltas plus 1 gripper command. The only arm slot is arm0; there is no second arm.

Frame franka_base is the URDF base_link panda_link0 (coincident with MuJoCo robot0_base), right-handed in metres and radians with +X pointing forward across the workspace, +Y left, and +Z straight up. In this single-arm simulation T_world_base equals identity in franka_base; note that in LIBERO tabletop suites (such as libero_spatial, libero_goal, and libero_10) the robot pedestal sits 0.012 m above the tabletop surface (table top z = -0.012 m in franka_base), whereas in floor suites (such as libero_object) the support surface lies at z = 0.000 m. Always read the observation's calibrated support/table height rather than assuming z = 0. All seven URDF revolute joint axes are local +Z of their joint frames, with fixed +-90 deg X rotations between consecutive joints and offsets 0.333 m (J1), 0.316 m (J3), (0.0825, 0, 0) m (J4), (-0.0825, 0.384, 0) m (J5), and (0.088, 0, 0) m (J7). Apply each parent-to-joint transform before joint motion; local axis signs alone are not world directions. LIBERO episodes spawn near nominal posture [0.0, -0.161, 0.0, -2.445, 0.0, 2.227, 0.7854] rad rather than all-zero joints.

The Cartesian TCP is URDF link gripper0_grip_site (libero-panda-grip-site-v1), fixed at +0.097 m along panda_hand local +Z (+0.2035 m along panda_link7 +Z, rotated by robosuite's wrist/gripper mount). gripper0_grip_site lies directly at the nominal grasp pad reference between the two fingers (grasp_reference_offset_m = 0.0 m). Local +Z of gripper0_grip_site is the outward approach axis along the fingers (pointing straight down [0, 0, -1] in franka_base at LIBERO spawn quaternion_xyzw [0.707107, 0.707107, 0, 0]); local +-X is the parallel-jaw opening/closing line (panda_rightfinger opens along local +X as panda_finger_joint2 moves 0..-0.04 m, and panda_leftfinger opens along local -X as panda_finger_joint1 increases 0..0.04 m); local +Y points toward the eye-in-hand wrist camera mounted at (0.0, +0.050, -0.097) m in gripper0_grip_site. Along local +Z relative to gripper0_grip_site, the exposed inner finger faces span -0.0445 m to +0.0093 m, the high-friction rubber pad collision boxes span -0.0116 m to +0.0044 m, pad_center is at 0.0000 m, finger_middle is at -0.0176 m (contact_depth_m = +0.0176 m), and the rigid distal fingertips reach +0.0093 m beyond gripper0_grip_site (contact_depth_m = -0.0093 m). Both direct TCP poses (position_m + quaternion_xyzw) and top-down grasp_angles (grasp_position_m + angles_deg = [pitch_deg, roll_deg, yaw_deg], where [0, 0, 0] points approach straight down -Z and opens jaws along world -X, and [0, 0, -90] opens jaws along world +Y matching spawn quaternion_xyzw [0.707107, 0.707107, 0, 0]) place the selected contact reference directly without double-adding any offset.

Motion stages drive robosuite's native 20 Hz OperationalSpaceController (OSC_POSE) directly in franka_base task space with gravity compensation and posture null-space damping; joint limits (especially panda_joint4 in [-3.0718, -0.0698] rad and panda_joint6 in [-0.0175, 3.7525] rad), wrist wrap, and workspace reach constrain achievable poses. Rotating or tilting the wrist sweeps panda_hand, the protruding wrist camera, and any held object through space; neither trajectory preview nor task-space tracking certifies full-body or held-object collision clearance unless explicitly checked.

Gripper command goal_normalized in [0, 1] is dimensionless: 0.0 requests fully closed and 1.0 requests fully open (0.080 m nominal span, targeting symmetric finger displacements +0.040 m and -0.040 m via closed-loop aperture control over PandaGripper's position actuators with kp = 1000 N/m and force range +-20 N). Measured aperture_m is qpos[0] + abs(qpos[1]) in metres. Because the two 4 mm half-thickness rubber pad collision boxes overlap by 0.5 mm each at q = 0, an empty full close settles at steady-state aperture_m = 0.0010 m (qpos = [+0.0005, -0.0005] m), while an empty full open reaches 0.0797..0.0800 m (pad inner surface gap = aperture_m - 0.0010 m, up to 0.0790 m). Non-zero aperture above 0.0010 m indicates incomplete motion or contact opposition, not guaranteed grasp retention.

The simulation runs in discrete steps at 20 Hz control frequency over a 0.002 s MuJoCo physics timestep (25 physics substeps per control step); static observations and reasoning do not advance physics. Two calibrated 512x512 pinhole cameras are published in OpenCV optical convention (+X right, +Y down, +Z forward): fixed overview camera agentview and eye-in-hand camera wrist (fovy = 75 deg). Always verify requested, planned, and achieved motion as well as object contact, lift, and final placement stability from fresh measured proprioception and camera observations. Simulation link masses sum to 18.52 kg (robosuite nominal parameters) and establish no physical hardware payload or force ratings.
